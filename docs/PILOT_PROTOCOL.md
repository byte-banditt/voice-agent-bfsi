# Human-run pilot protocol

Template only. Pilot activity and all pilot metrics are **NOT EXECUTED**. Use invented or explicitly consented participant data only; never use customer data without organizational approval.

## Consent script

“This is a prototype voice assistant for a research pilot. Participation is optional. Please do not share real account numbers or sensitive financial details. The prototype may store a redacted transcript and feedback for evaluation; raw audio is not stored by default. You may stop now or ask to withdraw your pilot record by contacting [owner/contact]. Do you consent to continue?”

Record consent outcome separately from transcript. If consent is not affirmative, end the session without collecting evaluation data.

## Data and withdrawal

Collect only call ID, consent state, redacted transcript, outcome, confusion flag, rating, and optional free text. Keep access restricted. Withdrawal request: locate by pilot ID, delete the row and associated trace, record deletion completion without retaining the participant's content.
